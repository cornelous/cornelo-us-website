Cornelo Google Drive Sync

Cornelo Google Drive Sync is a software project that uses rclone and Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication to allow users to synchronise files from their Google Drive accounts to storage on their own computers.

Authentication and file transfers take place directly between Google and the user's local computer. Cornelo does not operate a central server that receives or stores users' Google Drive files.

Users control their synchronisation configuration, local storage and Google account permissions.
