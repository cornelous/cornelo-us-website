2. Description of the software

Cornelo Google Drive Sync facilitates synchronisation between Google Drive and a user's local computer using rclone and Google's OAuth authentication services.

The software operates through the user's own computer and authorised Google Account. Cornelo does not provide a hosted file storage or synchronisation backend.

3. User responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Reviewing and approving requested Google permissions.
  • Protecting local OAuth credentials and configuration files.
  • Ensuring they have the necessary rights to access and synchronise their files.
  • Maintaining appropriate backups of important information.
  • Configuring synchronisation operations carefully to avoid unintended modification or deletion of files.
