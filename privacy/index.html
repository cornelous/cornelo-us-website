Privacy Policy

Application: Cornelo Google Drive Sync

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Cornelo ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when users authenticate with Google and synchronise Google Drive files using Cornelo Google Drive Sync.

2. How the application works

Cornelo Google Drive Sync uses rclone, an open-source command-line tool, to facilitate file synchronisation between Google Drive and a user's own computer.

Google OAuth 2.0 is used to authenticate users and obtain the permissions required to access Google Drive. Users must authorise access through Google's authentication process.

Authentication and file transfers take place directly between Google and the user's local computer. Cornelo does not operate a backend server that receives, processes or stores users' Google Drive file contents.

3. Information accessed

Depending on the permissions requested and granted by the user, the application may access Google Drive information necessary for synchronisation. This may include:

  • Google Drive file and folder names and metadata.
  • File contents selected for synchronisation.
  • File identifiers, sizes, modification dates and other metadata required by the synchronisation process.
  • OAuth authentication tokens used to maintain authorised access.

The application accesses only the Google account resources permitted by the OAuth scopes and permissions authorised by the user.

4. Use of Google user data

Google user data is used solely to provide the functionality requested by the user, including authentication, access to authorised Google Drive resources and file synchronisation.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except where necessary to provide the user-requested functionality through Google and the software components involved in synchronisation.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens used by rclone are stored in the user's local rclone configuration on their own computer. Synchronised files are stored in the local destination selected by the user.

Cornelo does not maintain a central database or cloud storage service containing users' Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Users are responsible for securing their computers, protecting their local configuration files and maintaining appropriate backups.

6. Data retention and deletion

Synchronised files remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them or otherwise manages their local storage. OAuth tokens remain in the local rclone configuration until revoked or deleted by the user.

Cornelo does not independently retain copies of synchronised Google Drive files or OAuth tokens on its own servers.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes. Data is accessed and transferred directly through Google's services and the locally operated synchronisation software.

8. Revoking access

Users may revoke the application's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security settings:

Manage third-party connections in Google Account

Users may also remove their local rclone configuration and associated authentication tokens from their computers.

9. Children's privacy

This application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data-handling practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
